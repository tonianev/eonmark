Size S · Risk low · Lane R · Waits on: nothing

The art pass's three and M6's two "Good first issues" exist on GitHub, so both milestones can close (ROADMAP "Closing a milestone", rule 5).

4. Append ` (#NN)` to each of the five bullets in the ROADMAP (they are not acceptance lines; `scripts/check_roadmap_ticks.py` ignores them).
