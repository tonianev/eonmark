# M6-2: Tuning round (0 to 3 of them)

Size M · Risk high · Lane S · Waits on: M6-1 finding a failure (conditional)

## Goal
Fix only the criteria the owner's matches failed, with data, keep every fun-gate line green, and give the owner new blind seeds for the replay of the three matches.

## Serves
M6 line 6, whose own text sets the rule: each failed criterion gets a `rules.ron` tuning commit (`data/ai` may ride along, M6 owner decision 11) and the three matches are played again; at most 3 rounds before an ADR on the ruleset. M6 owner decision 18 limits the scope to what the matches exposed.

## Read first
PLAYBOOK P4 (versions), P5 (fun gate), P6 (tuning budget), P7 (blind seeds). `docs/design/ai.md`, "Difficulty table (as retuned...)" and "Deviations pending the owner" items 3 to 5 (read by `grep -n '^#' docs/design/ai.md`).

## Anchors
- `data/ai/difficulty/standard.ron`: `wave_interval_permille`, `wave_size_permille`, `first_raid`, `commit_ds`, `reinforce_min_units`, `launch_ratio_permille`
- `data/rules/rules.ron`: `yield_cap_base`, `yield_cap_decay_after_ds`, `yield_cap_decay_step`, `harrying_bonus_hp`
- `data/ai/personalities.ron`: `rush`
- `scripts/fun_gate_all.sh`: `DATA`
- `scripts/same_outcomes.sh`: `final_hash`

## Steps
1. Write the hypothesis into the PR first: the failed criterion, the lever, the expected direction (P6.1).
2. Experiment on a copy: `cp -r data /tmp/td`, edit the copy, `DATA=/tmp/td scripts/fun_gate_all.sh all /tmp/fg_<variant>` (about 10 minutes; P6). At most six variants per session; each is one row of a table you commit under `docs/design/ai.md`, "Tuning round N".
3. Levers, in this order (read `data/ai/README.md` for a field before editing it):

   | Failed criterion | First lever | Second lever |
   |---|---|---|
   | The AI's army never reached the player's borders, or no Town fell below 50% HP, in 2 of 3 | `standard.ron` `wave_interval_permille` 1000 to 850 | `pressure.commit_ds` 300 to 450 |
   | A match shorter than 20 minutes because the bot won | `standard.ron` `first_raid.by_ds` 9000 to 10800 | `wave_size_permille` 1000 to 900 |
   | A match shorter than 20 minutes because the owner won | `pressure.reinforce_min_units` 6 to 4 | `wave_size_permille` 1000 to 1150 |
   | A match longer than 40 minutes | `rules.ron` `yield_cap_decay_step` 0 to 1 (decay from 30:00) | `harrying_bonus_hp` 0 to 2 |
   | The Yield Cap readout never turned amber | `rules.ron` `yield_cap_base` 70 to 62 | none |
   | Fun below 3 | the lever the owner's notes point to | none: ask the owner which row to try |
   | One style's match failed | that style's gap in ai.md "Deviations" items 3 and 4, in `data/ai/personalities.ron` or `data/ai/build_orders/<style>.ron` | none |

   A lever that moves a fun-gate line is measured against all of them before it ships (line 2's first clause rides on Standard's launch gate).
4. Ship the best variant that passes every fun-gate line: one `rules_version` bump (P4; a `crates/ai` change also bumps `SIM_VERSION`), `just rerecord`, `just verify`.
5. New blind seeds (P7) into PLAYTEST's seed table; ask the owner for O1 again with `iteration N` in the Notes.

## Verify
P6's table in the PR; `scripts/fun_gate_all.sh all` green on the shipped data; `just verify`; `just fixture-policy origin/main`; CI green.

## Done when
The shipped variant passes the fun gate's CI lines and its table is committed.

## Stop if
Round 3 still fails a criterion: open a `needs-design` issue proposing the ADR on the ruleset that the line requires. Or no variant within the budget passes both the criterion's proxy and every fun-gate line: post the table and the two best options for the owner.
